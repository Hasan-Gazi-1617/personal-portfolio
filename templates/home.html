THE ENGINEERING MINDSET

Reliable networks start with clear thinking.

My approach combines practical ISP operations with careful troubleshooting and useful automation—so the goal is not just to restore a service, but to understand why it failed.

01 / OBSERVE

See the full picture.

Correlate alarms, optical levels, interface counters and customer impact before jumping to a conclusion.

02 / UNDERSTAND

Find the real cause.

Trace symptoms across access, aggregation and core layers to separate the root cause from the visible symptom.

03 / IMPROVE

Make the fix repeatable.

Document what worked, reduce repetitive effort and turn recurring operational tasks into better tools and workflows.
